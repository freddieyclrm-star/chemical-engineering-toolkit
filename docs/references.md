# Reference
## Energy Balance
### **Smith, Van Ness & Abbott — Introduction to Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics (2005)**
Used for: sensible heat duty, latent heat transfer, reaction enthalpy, general energy balances, heat exchanger energy balance.
### **Çengel & Boles — Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach**
Used for: sensible heat, phase change thermodynamics, Cp assumptions, steady‑state energy balance.
### **Moran & Shapiro — Fundamentals of Engineering Thermodynamics**
Used for: first‑law energy balance, enthalpy definitions, closed/open system energy equations.


## Mass Balance
### **Himmelblau — Basic Principles and Calculations in Chemical Engineering**
Used for: mass flow definitions, density–volumetric flow relationships, multi‑stream balances.
### **Felder & Rousseau — Elementary Principles of Chemical Processes (2005)**
Used for: single‑stream mass balance, multi‑stream mass balance, steady‑state assumptions.
### **Cengel, Y. A., & Boles, M. A. (2015). Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach**
Used in Single stream balance, net mass balance for multiple streams, mass flow via density and volumetric flow
### **Bird, Stewart & Lightfoot — Transport Phenomena (2002)**
Used for: mass flux, density relationships, transport‑based mass flow derivations.


## Reaction & Stoichiometry
### **Fogler, H. S. (2016). Elements of Chemical Reaction Engineering.**
Used for: stoichiometric relations, reaction rate definitions, extent of reaction.
### **Levenspiel — Chemical Reaction Engineering**
Used for: stoichiometric coefficients, conversion, yield, selectivity.
### **Atkins & de Paula — Physical Chemistry**
Used for: reaction enthalpy, thermochemical data, molecular properties.


## Engineering Constants
### **NIST Chemistry WebBook**
Used for: atmospheric pressure, molar masses, Cp values, thermochemical data.
### **CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics**
Used for: density of pure water at 4 °C, specific heat capacity of water, standard physical constants.
### **ICAO Standard Atmosphere**
Used for: density of dry air at sea level, standard atmospheric conditions.
### **IAPWS (International Association for the Properties of Water and Steam)**
Used for: water/steam thermodynamic properties, density, enthalpy, saturation data.


## Scientific constants
### **CODATA 2018 (NIST)**
Used for:

- speed of light

- Planck constant

- Boltzmann constant

- Newton’s gravitational constant

- Stefan–Boltzmann constant

- elementary charge

- electron/proton/neutron mass

- Avogadro’s constant

- molar gas constant
